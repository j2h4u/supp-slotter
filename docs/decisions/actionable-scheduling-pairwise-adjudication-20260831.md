## Future pairwise admission is deliberately narrow

A future relation may become a `pressure` only after all of the following are
separately accepted and verified:

1. A minimal typed **world fact/mechanism**, not a conclusion such as
   `same_slot`, `different_slot`, `prefer_same_slot`,
   `prefer_different_slot`, a desired placement, an action, or presentation
   prose.
2. Exact typed source and target applicability at stable substance or
   composition-role identity, plus provenance for that exact scope.
3. One closed, identity-free universal law whose result derives the resulting
   pressure. The law may not name products, items, slots, conditions, weights,
   explanations, or a relation-specific recommendation.
4. A contradiction rule: opposing pairwise-derived values for the same item and
   dimension are layout-free `Indeterminate`, never a choice, cancellation,
   average, weighted vote, or balance outcome.
5. Compiler/inference, exact-optimizer, and active-shelf coverage evidence
   required by the governing V contracts.

Evidence strength, research state, source count, and witness count remain
provenance/explanation metadata only. They never create a numeric weight,
multiply a pressure, or let an otherwise passive relation influence placement.

## Explicit historical-advisory prohibition

Historical advisory constraints or generated residues must **not** be restored
as pairwise scheduling behavior. In particular, Calcium--Zinc,
Calcium--Manganese, Calcium--Iron, and Zinc--Copper cannot regain a score delta,
hard block, soft penalty, or stored separation preference merely because an
older artifact or UI wording said "prefer separate." Their current dispositions
above control until the future-admission conditions are met.

This also prohibits an indirect compatibility path: no adapter may translate a
passive `supports`, `balance`, or `co_use_context` record into pairwise
placement behavior. A review explanation may state that a passive relation had
no scheduling effect, but it is derived output, not canonical input.
