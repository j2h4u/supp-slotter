# Actionable Scheduling Knowledge Coverage Boundary — 2026-08-31

## Status

**Accepted.** This decision governs the recovery's semantic-coverage V-left
contract. It supplements the [canonical instance and inference
boundary](canonical-instance-inference-boundary-20260822.md), which remains the
governing facts-only and exact-optimizer decision, and is elaborated in the
living [domain model](../domain-model.md).

## Context

The completed runtime cutover proved a closed catalog, universal normalization
laws, exact global optimization, and fail-closed publication. Its final
convergence and fresh-context audit did not establish that the real active
shelf was semantically covered: an active card could retain prose, a structured
membership, or a passive relation that never became scheduler-visible input.
The earlier V-model had no upper contract requiring every active role and
discovered candidate to be assessed, so lower mechanism gates could pass while
knowledge was not accountable to the product outcome.

## Decision

### Product and scope invariants

The product publishes only a globally proved `Optimal` layout or layout-free
`Indeterminate`. For the active shelf, an optimal layout is publishable only
when its semantic coverage is complete. Slots remain unbounded logical intake
groups; this decision adds no capacity, tablet/capsule count, mass, volume, or
physical-fit semantics. It adds no dosage, diagnosis, treatment, clinical
safety advice, general rule engine, NLP/prose interpretation, UI framework,
migration runtime, compatibility adapter, or legacy museum.

The exact lexicographic objective is unchanged: maximize satisfied unique
pressures, then minimize exact integer squared load, then use the stable
assignment tie-break. Only proven global optimality may render a layout.

### Closed coverage and disposition vocabulary

Every source-indexed scheduling candidate discovered for an active daily,
episodic, or training composition role has exactly one disposition:

| Disposition | Closed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pressure` | An individually adjudicated directed hypothesis with typed subject/applicability, provenance/research state, an admitted fact or pairwise world-fact mechanism, and a universal-law path. |
| `neutral` | An assessed in-model candidate with no directed scheduling outcome. |
| `unresolved_without_direction` | Evidence is incomplete and does not justify a directed hypothesis. It is research-open but coverage-closed for this exact candidate once its scope, provenance, and disposition validate. |
| `outside_model` | An explicitly reasoned closed exclusion with no scheduling effect. |

No candidate may be missing, duplicated, mutually inconsistent, prose-only, or
closed by a blanket `neutral`/`outside_model` decision. A valid
`unresolved_without_direction` disposition closes coverage without becoming
neutral; missing, unassessed, malformed, or stale coverage does not. This ADR
does not pre-adjudicate any actual candidate.

After individual expert adjudication, every directed hypothesis—including
anecdotal, mechanistic, or weak evidence—is one unweighted formal soft pressure
derived through a typed fact and universal law. Evidence strength and research
state are provenance/explanation metadata, never a numeric objective weight;
sources, components, witnesses, and proof paths do not multiply a pressure.
Thus an admitted pressure outranks balance under the unchanged exact objective.

### Pairwise, contradiction, and notes boundaries

Pairwise co-location or separation may be derived only from a minimal typed
world fact/relation and a universal law. A stored `same_slot`, `different_slot`,
preference, placement, action, or semantic UI sentence is a stored answer, not
a canonical input. Passive supports, balance, and contextual relations stay
passive unless a separately accepted mechanism and law entail a pressure.

Opposing values for the same item and dimension, including pairwise-derived
values, are a direct contradiction. They return layout-free `Indeterminate`
and require adjudication; they cannot be selected, canceled, averaged,
weighted, or optimized around.

Generic authored `notes` must be structurally removed from canonical product,
substance, and component cards. A one-time stable source-span inventory may
move material to a typed identity, composition, provenance, candidate/evidence
claim, or explicit discard reason. After cutover `notes` fail schema; no parser,
heuristic, compatibility field, migration runtime, or museum remains.

### Certificate and V-model requirements

A coverage certificate is derived verification metadata, not an optimizer input
or stored answer. Per active composition role, it records applicable dimensions,
the exhaustive evaluated candidate IDs, exactly-one dispositions, each
disposition's required evidence path, and exact input hashes. A valid
`unresolved_without_direction` entry remains explicit as research-open and
coverage-closed; it is neither neutral nor a pressure. The certificate proves
that no unadjudicated directional candidate was omitted, not that no admitted
pressure exists; a balance-and-tie-break-only explanation exposes unsatisfied
admitted pressures. Only a complete current certificate permits a balance-only
outcome. Missing, unassessed, stale, or malformed coverage is layout-free
`Indeterminate`.

| V level | V-left contract | V-right acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product invariant | Real active-shelf scheduling materially follows formal world facts. | A real-shelf trace proves every placement and every balance-only certificate. |
| Knowledge coverage | Every active role and discovered candidate has one formal disposition; canonical cards have no generic notes. | Deterministic manifest: zero orphan/prose-only or unassessed candidates; schema/corpus/source-span receipts prove notes removal without loss. |
| Ontology/admission | Directed hypotheses use typed facts or minimal pairwise world facts plus universal laws. | Compiler/inference proof covers applicability, provenance, dimension, value, and law; passive-relation negative tests pass. |
| Objective | Each pressure outranks balance; exact global optimizer is unchanged. | Metamorphic and exhaustive witnesses prove pressure-before-balance and exactness. |
| Publication | Incomplete coverage and direct contradictions are layout-free. | `Indeterminate` witnesses reject absent/stale certificates and contradictions; certified balance-only explanations are complete. |
| Workflow | Prose, notes, and passive relations cannot close grooming. | Workflow smoke and schema rejection leave a candidate open until its typed disposition/evidence exists. |

## Consequences

Semantic coverage becomes a product-level requirement before any new
admission/runtime work begins. The next work must inventory and individually
adjudicate candidates; it must not infer scheduling semantics from prose or
pre-classify the named recovery witnesses. Implementation can only proceed
down the V after the paired contracts are accepted, and must ascend through the
matrix's real active-shelf evidence before claiming recovery completion.
